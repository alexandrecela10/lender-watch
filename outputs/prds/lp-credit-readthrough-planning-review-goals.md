# Goals: Lender Watch PRD, Planning Review

**Output:** `lp-credit-readthrough-planning-review.md`
**Passing bar:** all criteria pass before `/prd-review-panel` findings are applied or the build starts.

| # | Exit criterion (binary) |
|---|---|
| 1 | The opening states what goes in, what comes out, for whom, the gain and what it can't do yet. |
| 2 | The problem table matches the opportunity analysis, with one hypothesis and one metric with a target per row. |
| 3 | Every number is marked or traceable as observed or assumed. |
| 4 | The primary metric has a baseline, a target and a date. |
| 5 | Kill criteria name a specific condition and a specific action. |
| 6 | The AI contract has match, no_match and unsure examples taken from real filing text. |
| 7 | Every open question has an owner. |
| 8 | No em dashes, banned words or sales phrases. |
| 9 | Main body at or under about 800 words, excluding tables and appendices. |
