# Goals: spec for AI drift tracking

Passing bar: all 6 criteria are true.

1. The bottom line says, in plain words, what runs, when, what fails, what it costs and what it sends out.
2. Every concern has a decision and a reason, and none leaves a number invented (variation stays unobserved until measured).
3. Each problem has one metric with a target, and the table follows the house format.
4. Every acceptance check can pass or fail with no judgement call, and none calls Anthropic except the one marked manual.
5. The spec names what is not tackled, each with what's missing.
6. No company name from a private list, and no organisation Alexandre is applying to, appears in it.
